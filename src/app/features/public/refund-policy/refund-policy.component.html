Overview

At Braseth Printing Shop, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not happy with your order, we are here to help. This policy outlines the conditions under which refunds and returns are processed.

Eligibility for Refunds

You may request a refund under the following conditions:

  • The item was received damaged or defective
  • The item does not match the description or product listing
  • The wrong item was delivered
  • The order was not delivered within the guaranteed timeframe
  • Standard (non-custom) items returned unused in original packaging within 14 days of delivery

Custom Printed Items

Due to the personalized nature of custom-printed products, they cannot be returned or refunded unless:

  • There is a manufacturing defect (misprints, incorrect colors, fabric damage)
  • The printed design does not match the approved proof
  • The wrong size or product type was shipped

Please review your design proof carefully before confirming your order. Once production begins, custom orders cannot be cancelled.

How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund, follow these steps:

  • Step 1: Log in to your account and navigate to your order history
  • Step 2: Select the order and click "Request Refund"
  • Step 3: Provide a reason for the refund and attach any supporting photos
  • Step 4: Our team will review your request within 2 business days
  • Step 5: If approved, follow the return instructions provided (if applicable)

Alternatively, you can contact support at orders@braseth.com with your order number and reason for the refund.

Refund Processing

Once your return is received and inspected, or your refund is approved (for cases not requiring a return), we will process your refund:

  • Processing Time: 5-7 business days after approval
  • Refund Method: Original payment method used for the purchase
  • Partial Refunds: May be issued for items returned in non-original condition
  • Shipping Costs: Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error

Exchanges

We offer exchanges for standard products within 14 days of delivery. Exchanges are subject to product availability. If the desired replacement is unavailable, a full refund will be issued instead.
